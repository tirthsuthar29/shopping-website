Returns & Refunds

We want you to be perfectly satisfied with your purchase.

Our 14-Day Return Policy

You have 14 calendar days to return an item from the date you received it. To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused, in the same condition that you received it, and must be in the original packaging.

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact our support team through the Contact Us page.
  2. Provide your Order Tracking ID and the reason for the return.
  3. We will provide you with a shipping label and instructions within 24 hours.

Refunds Process

Once we receive your item, we will inspect it and notify you that we have received your returned item. If your return is approved, we will initiate a refund to your original method of payment. You will receive the credit within 3-5 business days, depending on your card issuer's policies.
