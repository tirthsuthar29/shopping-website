Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to NOVATECH. We are committed to protecting your personal data and your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the data necessary to provide you with a seamless shopping experience:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication data (via Firebase Auth).
  • Shopping Data: Cart contents, wishlist items, and product comparisons.
  • Order History: Shipping addresses, contact numbers, and order details (stored securely in Firestore).
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, and login history for security purposes.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders.
  • Syncing your cart and wishlist across your devices.
  • Improving our store layout and product recommendations.
  • Providing customer support and responding to reviews.

4. Data Storage & Security

We use industry-standard security measures provided by Google Firebase. Your data is stored in secure cloud servers with end-to-end encryption. We do not store your credit card numbers on our servers; payments are processed through secure simulated gateways.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use local storage and essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember your shopping cart. No invasive third-party tracking cookies are used for advertising.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data at any time through your Profile page or by contacting our support team.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at:
